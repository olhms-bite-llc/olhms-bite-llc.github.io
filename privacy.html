Privacy policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

This policy covers OHLMS BITE Publisher, an internal tool run by OHLMS BITE LLC to post our own videos to our own social media accounts.

Who uses it

Only OHLMS BITE staff, signing in to accounts that OHLMS BITE owns. Members of the public do not sign in to it.

What it accesses

When we connect one of our accounts, the platform (for example Google/YouTube or Meta) gives the tool a token that lets it upload videos and read basic details of that account, such as the channel or page name. It does not read viewers' personal data, messages or contacts.

How the data is used and stored

Tokens are used only to upload our approved videos and to check that the connection still works. They are stored encrypted in our private code repository's secret store and are never sold, shared or used for advertising.

Google user data

Our use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. The tool uses the YouTube Data API; see the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Removing access

An account owner can remove the tool's access at any time: for Google at myaccount.google.com/permissions, and for Meta in Business settings. When access is removed, we delete the stored token.
